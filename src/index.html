01 About Kumasi · Accra · München

Supply chains are where economics, engineering and human needs collide. That's exactly where I work.

I'm an early-career researcher committed to advancing supply chain knowledge through rigorous, impactful and collaborative research. My Master's at KNUST examines how supply chain intelligence and knowledge networks drive business model innovation. My field work has taken me from Kumasi's informal freight corridors to Germany's DAAD-funded research networks.

Before research became full-time, I was on the operational side: clearing imports at DHL Global Forwarding and tightening stock accuracy at Akosombo Hotels. That ground-level view keeps my academic work honest. Every model has to survive contact with a real container, a real customs officer and a real deadline.
